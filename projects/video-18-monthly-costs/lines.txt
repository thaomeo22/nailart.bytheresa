Aware of what it costs every month before your business makes profit.
Hi, I'm Theresa, a salon owner in Toronto. A lot of you've been curious about this.
So here is where your money goes. First, rent, TMI and HST, $5 ,000 to $20 ,000 a month.
Second, technician commission or payroll, 40 % to 55 % of the service revenue.
Third, receptionist and salon manager, $3 ,500 to $9 ,000 a month.
Four, nail supplies and disposable products, 5 % to 12 % of the revenue.
Fifth, electricity, gas, water, and hot water, $700 to $2 ,000 a month.
Six, internet, phone, and security, $200 to $600 a month.
Seven, booking platform and POS, $200 to $1 ,000 a month.
Eight, card processing fee, 1% to 3 .5% of card sales.
Nine, business insurance, $50 to $500 a month.
Ten, cleaning, laundry, and garbage, $400 to $1 ,500 a month.
Eleven, marketing, $500 to $3 ,000 a month.
Twelve, accounting, bookkeeping, and payroll, $300 to $1 ,200 a month.
Thirteen, repairs and equipment replacement, set aside $300 to $1 ,500 a month.
Fourteen, bank fees, refund, and chargebacks, $100 to $800 a month.
These are estimates and every salon is different.
