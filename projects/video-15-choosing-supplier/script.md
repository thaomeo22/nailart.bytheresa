## Script ghi âm
The cheapest supplier isn't always the cheapest.

Hi, I'm Theresa, a salon owner in Toronto. After I shared my list of nail supply stores, a lot of you asked how I choose. Here are my four checks.

One: consistency. Same product, same quality, every order. One bad batch of gel means lifting — and redo appointments.

Two: delivery time. If you run out of top coat on a Friday, can they get it to you before Saturday?

Three: returns and support. Broken bottles, wrong colours — how fast do they fix it?

Four: the real price. Compare the cost per service, not per bottle — and add shipping and minimum orders.

I keep one main supplier and one backup for every key product.

Follow me for more salon business tips.
