The cheapest booking app can be the most expensive one.
Hi, I'm Theresa, a salon owner in Toronto.
Here are 7 things to check before you choose your booking system.
The last one is extremely important, so stick around to the end.
1. Is it easy for clients to book? Can they finish in under a minute?
2. Is it easy for you and your staff? Can you manage it from your phone anywhere?
3. Can you set service time for each technician?
A new tech may need more time than an experienced one, right?
4. Does it have reminders, deposit, and cancellation policy to reduce no -shows?
5. Can it calculate commission, check tips separately, and export payroll reports?
6. What is the total cost? Add up monthly fee, staff fee,
text message, card processing, or even new client fee.
7. Ask for a demo at least 2 -week trial. Test it on quiet days and busy days.
Does it actually make your salon easier to manage?
