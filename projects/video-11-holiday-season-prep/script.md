## Script ghi âm
November and December are our busiest months. Is your salon ready?

Hi, I'm Theresa, a salon owner in Toronto. Here's how I prepare.

One: open holiday bookings early, and take a deposit for peak days — so a last-minute no-show doesn't cost you a full chair.

Two: plan your team's schedule now. Extra shifts, time-off requests, and who covers the busiest Saturdays.

Three: sell gift cards. They're cash in December and new clients in January. And in Ontario, most retail gift cards can't have an expiry date or extra fees — so set them up the right way.

Four: stock up early. Holiday colours, top coat and files run out fast, and deliveries slow down in December.

Plan now, and the holidays feel busy — not chaotic.

Follow me for more salon business tips.
