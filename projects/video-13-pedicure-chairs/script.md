## Script ghi âm
Pipeless or jetted? The pedicure chair you buy decides how much cleaning you do — every single day.

Hi, I'm Theresa, a salon owner in Toronto. Here's what to think about.

Jetted chairs have pipes and a recirculation system. Under Toronto Public Health guidelines, between clients you clean the basin, run detergent through the jets, then circulate disinfectant for the full contact time. And every night, the filter comes out with a screwdriver to be cleaned and disinfected.

Pipeless basins: clean with soap and water, rinse, and disinfect after every client. No pipes for bacteria to hide in.

Why does it matter? In 2000, one California salon had 110 clients with skin infections — traced back to bacteria growing in its whirlpool footbaths.

So before you buy, check the cleaning steps — not just the price and the massage.
