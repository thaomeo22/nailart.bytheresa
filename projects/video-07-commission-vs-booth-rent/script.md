## Script ghi âm
Commission or booth rent? Choosing wrong can cost you your best nail tech.

Hi, I'm Theresa, a salon owner in Toronto. At my salons, our techs are on commission — 45 to 55 percent. Here's why.

With commission, you control the quality. Same menu, same prices, same standards — and clients book the salon, not just one tech.

Booth rent looks easier: steady rent, less to manage. But the tech runs her own business — her prices, her hours, her clients. When she leaves, her clients leave with her.

And here's the trap: calling someone a booth renter doesn't make them one. If you set their hours and prices and take their payments, the CRA or Ontario's Ministry of Labour may see them as an employee — and you could owe back pay.

Building a brand? Commission. Just renting out space? Booth rent — and keep it truly independent.

Follow me for more salon business tips.

## Caption đăng
Commission keeps quality and clients with your salon. Booth rent gives techs independence — but only if they're truly independent. In Ontario, a "booth renter" you control like an employee may legally be an employee.
