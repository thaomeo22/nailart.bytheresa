## Script ghi âm (bản chủ kênh chốt 27/10)
Are you disinfecting your nail tools properly?

Hey, I'm Theresa. Fix Your Foundation, episode 38: how to actually clean and disinfect your tools.

First, clean. Brush out all the dust and gel, then wash with soap and water. Disinfectant can't work through debris.

Second, rinse and dry. Air dry, or pat dry with a clean single-use towel.

Third, disinfect. Soak the whole bit for 10 minutes — in a disinfectant with a DIN on the label, 70 percent isopropyl alcohol, or 20 mL of bleach in 1 litre of water, made fresh every day.

Then store them in a clean, covered container. The UV box is only for storing bits that are already disinfected.

Do this between every client. Keep two or three sets of your favourite bits, and it's easy.

Follow Fix Your Foundation to master every technique fundamental. I'm Theresa — see you.

## Caption đăng
Clean with soap and water, rinse and dry, then soak for 10 minutes in an intermediate-level disinfectant with a DIN, 70% isopropyl alcohol, or a fresh 1:50 bleach solution (20 mL bleach in 1 L water). Store covered — a UV box is storage, not disinfection. Between every client, so keep a few sets of your favourite bits.

## Ghi chú
Hook trong Doc vẫn là "Brushing your bits isn't cleaning them" và script vẫn nói "Soak the whole bit… bits" — đề xuất đổi hook thành "Are you disinfecting your nail tools properly?" và "bit" → "tool" cho khớp tiêu đề mới. Public Health Ontario ghi bleach ăn mòn kim loại nếu ngâm lâu → với đầu mài không ngâm quá 10 phút, rửa lại bằng nước sạch và lau khô; cồn 70% nhẹ hơn cho đầu mài.

## Gợi ý cảnh quay
Bàn chải làm sạch bụi trên đầu mài (macro), rửa xà phòng ở bồn rửa dụng cụ, lau khô bằng khăn dùng 1 lần, thả đầu mài ngập trong hộp cồn 70% hoặc dung dịch bleach (đồng hồ 10 phút), xếp vào hộp kín, hộp UV chỉ để cất, 2–3 bộ đầu mài.

## Nguồn
- Ontario Regulation 136/18 — s.10(4) (reusable equipment cleaned and disinfected between each use), s.11(2) (disinfectant with Health Canada DIN, used per manufacturer's instructions): https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/180136
- Toronto Public Health — Manicures and Pedicures: Infection Prevention and Control (drill bits: clean with soap & water, rinse, dry, soak whole instrument in intermediate-level disinfectant with DIN/NPN, e.g. 70% isopropyl alcohol for 10 minutes; store in clean covered container; UV units not approved for disinfection): https://www.toronto.ca/community-people/health-wellness-care/health-programs-advice/bodysafe/about-bodysafe/manicures-and-pedicures-infection-prevention-and-control/
- Public Health Ontario — Infection Prevention and Control in Personal Service Settings, 3rd ed. (2019), Appendix E: 1:50 household bleach (5.25%) = 1,000 ppm, intermediate-level, 10 minutes; make fresh daily; corrodes metal with prolonged soaking: https://www.publichealthontario.ca/-/media/documents/G/2019/guide-ipac-personal-service-settings.pdf
