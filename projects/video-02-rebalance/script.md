Hey, I'm Theresa. Fix Your Foundation, episode 35. How to rebalance a grown-out set, in three steps.

First, file it back. Thin out the old product and blend the regrowth line, so you're not stacking new gel on a thick, heavy base.

Second, prep the new growth. Cut the cuticle before or after — your choice. But the cuticle must be pushed back, and the nail surface cleaned, before any gel touches it.

Third, move the apex back. As the nail grows, the apex slides toward the tip — and that's where it breaks. Rebuild it over the stress area. With Dual Form and the right form, this is so much faster than sculpting gel by hand.

File, prep, rebuild. Faster fills that actually last.

## Ghi chú
Thứ tự 3 bước: mình đổi thành File → Prep → Rebuild apex (bạn ghi Prep là bước 3, nhưng prep phải làm trước khi đắp gel). Ý Dual Form lấy từ ghi chú của bạn.

## Gợi ý cảnh quay
Bộ móng mọc dài ~3 tuần (cận cảnh vùng regrowth) → file mỏng lớp cũ → đẩy cuticle, lau mặt móng → đặt gel lên dual form, ấn form → kết quả nhìn ngang thấy apex.
