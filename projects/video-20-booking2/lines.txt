I spent $20 ,000 learning how to choose a booking platform.
Hi, I'm Theresa, a salon owner in Toronto.
This is my true story, and I hope you can learn from my experience.
When I just opened my first salon, I picked a famous, super smooth platform, and I paid
between $400 to $800 a month.
They charged a subscription, a fee per staff member, and a fee for every new client
from the marketplace.
About 8 months ago, something felt off.
Nearly all my clients were coming from the marketplace.
Even though Google, our website, and social media were working fine, I pushed back.
But they insisted that all those clients were the ones they had brought for me.
It took me almost 2 months to collect and present proof, until they finally admitted
the mistake and refunded me.
They even offered me a free year on the app, but I couldn't trust them with my
business or my client data anymore.
I switched to another platform, slow, buggy, and my clients keep having trouble booking.
It was the hardest time in my business.
I lost trust in every app out there.
Then a friend in IT offered me to build one specifically for my salon.
After many rounds of fine -tuning, clients now book fast, no hidden fees,
my team schedule easily, and I can check appointments from my phone, anywhere.
That friend is now the founder of SalonPilot.
If you're looking for booking apps, check them out, and compare a few others too.
I hope you find the right fit for your salon.
Thank you for listening to my story, and follow me for more salon business tips.
See ya!
