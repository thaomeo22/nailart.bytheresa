Hey, I'm Theresa. Fix Your Foundation, episode 36: heat spikes.

When gel cures, the molecules link together, and every link releases a little heat. More gel at once means more heat at once.

So first: work in thin layers. Build your structure in two or three thin layers, not one thick bead.

Second: flash cure. Put the hand in for a few seconds, take it out, then go back for the full cure. The heat comes out slower.

Third: use the lamp your gel was made for. The wrong lamp can cure too fast, or not enough.

And if your client says it's hot — let her take her hand out. Never ask her to push through it.

Follow Fix Your Foundation to master every technique fundamental. I'm Theresa — see you.
