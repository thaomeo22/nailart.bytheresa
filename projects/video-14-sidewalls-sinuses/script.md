## Script ghi âm
Messy sidewalls ruin a perfect set — and that's exactly where most techs cut the skin.

Hey, I'm Theresa. Fix Your Foundation, episode 41: working in the sinuses — the side grooves of the nail.

First: push back and lift. Use your pusher to lift the dead skin out of the side groove before any bit goes in.

Second: pick a small bit — a thin flame or a small ball — so it fits the groove without touching living skin.

Third: low speed, light pressure, and follow the curve of the nail. The bit should glide, not grab. You're removing dead skin only — never the living skin.

Fourth: angle the bit along the nail, not into the skin.

Clean sinuses mean your gel can go right to the edge — and stay there.

Follow Fix Your Foundation to master every technique fundamental. I'm Theresa — see you.
