## Script ghi âm
Your brown and nude gel wrinkles under the lamp? It's not the colour's fault.

Hey, I'm Theresa. Fix Your Foundation, episode 39: why dark fall shades wrinkle — and how to stop it.

Dark, heavily pigmented gels block UV light. The top cures, but the layer underneath stays soft — so the surface shrinks and wrinkles. And extra time in the lamp won't fix it, because the light can't reach the bottom.

So, first: go thin. Two or three thin coats instead of one thick one.

Second: cure every coat fully, with the lamp your gel was made for.

Third: if it wrinkles, don't cover it with another coat. Take it off and redo it. That soft gel underneath will lift — and uncured gel on the skin is an allergy risk.

Thin coats, full cure, no shortcuts.

Follow Fix Your Foundation to master every technique fundamental. I'm Theresa — see you.
