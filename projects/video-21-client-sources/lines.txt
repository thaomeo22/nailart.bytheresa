How to find clients? Hi, I'm Theresa, a salon owner in Toronto, and I group client sources
into 4 buckets, those help me have enough revenue for 2 nail salons now. First is search engine
optimization. Someone typing "nail salon near me" is ready to book. Keep your real nail
photos, price list, opening hours and booking link up to date. Second is social media. These
clients won't book right away, they're checking whether your work looks good. Post real
nails, and if you can, tag the technician and the location so clients can find you.
Third is ads. They bring clients in fast, which is great if you just open or have empty slots,
but don't just look at views. Measure your cost per booking. And fourth, the cheapest one,
is from your old clients. If you can, rebook them before they leave the salon, and when
they refer a new client to you, thank them with a small gift or discount. Ask every
client "How did you hear about us?" It's a great way to know which source is worth your
money. Follow me for more content about salon business. Bye!
