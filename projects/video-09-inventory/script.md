Hi, I'm Theresa, a salon owner in Toronto. Here's my simple inventory system.

First, split your stock in two: everyday items — base, top, builder gel, files, buffers — and seasonal items, like new colours and charms.

Second, set a minimum for every everyday item. When you hit that number, you reorder. No guessing.

Third, count once a week. Same day, same time.

Fourth, track usage per service. Know roughly how many clients one bottle of base gets you, and you'll know exactly what to order next month.

Less money sitting on shelves — and no more "we're out of top coat" on a Saturday.

Follow me for more salon business tips.

## Caption đăng
Everyday vs seasonal stock, a minimum level for each item, one weekly count, and usage per service — a simple system so you stop overbuying and stop running out.
