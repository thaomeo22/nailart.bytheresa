## Script ghi âm
Too fast, and you burn your client. Too slow, and the bit grabs. So what's the right speed?

Hey, I'm Theresa. Fix Your Foundation, episode 40: e-file speed for every bit.

First: there's no one magic number. Every machine and every bit is different — so start with the range on the bit's label.

Second: cuticle work with diamond or flame bits — go low. Around 5,000 to 10,000 RPM. Light pressure, and let the bit glide.

Third: removing gel with a carbide bit — go higher. Around 15,000 to 25,000 RPM. Keep the bit moving, and never stay in one spot.

Fourth: if your client feels heat, don't press harder. Lower the speed, lift, and move.

Right bit, right speed, light hands.

Follow Fix Your Foundation to master every technique fundamental. I'm Theresa — see you.

## Caption đăng
There's no single "right" e-file speed — but there is a right range for each job. Low speed and light pressure for cuticle work with diamond or flame bits, higher speed for gel removal with carbide, and always keep the bit moving. Check your bit's label first.
